# The Weakest Kakeya Improvement

Let

```math
h_0=3+\frac{\sqrt{17665}-97}{600}.
```

The conjecture is the following disjunction:

1. there is an $\eta\gt0$ such that every Kakeya set in $\mathbb R^4$ has
   Hausdorff dimension at least $h_0+\eta$; or
2. there is one fixed finite $q\ge3$ such that $A_3(q)$ holds, with the notation
   of the [folder README](README.md).

The first alternative asks for any strict improvement over the best published
four-dimensional Hausdorff exponent.  The second asks that the density loss in
the 2025 three-dimensional shaded-tube theorem be bounded by one exponent
independent of $\varepsilon$; it does not ask for the sharp exponent $3$.

This is the folder's unique moving **Weakest** statement.  Its provisional
openness and the rejected weakenings are recorded in the
[source audit](sources_and_implications.md#weakening-audit).
