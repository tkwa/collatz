# Development Record

## Governing criteria

The live development guide was read before branch work.  The benchmark must
start at current theorem strength, make every positive row open, use only
checked implications, provide at least two progress doublings, geometrically
space cumulative effort below score `10`, and redesign any integer contour
whose estimated skip probability exceeds `50%`.

## Weakening queue

- Recover the complete Li--Yang theorem, including the exact $o(n)$ loss.
- Test loss-only, coefficient-only, infinitely-often, explicitness, and
  language-class weakenings.
- Reject non-explicit existence statements, because Shannon counting makes
  them theorems and removes the benchmark's core difficulty.
- Continue until no weaker open candidate has at least a 20% chance of
  removing a core obstacle, then perform the required frontier hostile check.

## Strengthening queue

- Audit standard exponential circuit-size hypotheses and whether their
  witnesses lie in $E$, $NP$, or a larger class.
- Test eventual versus infinitely-often variants, encodings, padding, and
  completeness transfers.
- Red-team falsity, independence, undecidability, natural-proofs and
  algebrization barriers, and the requirement that the endpoint remain at
  least 80% likely to be provable in accepted foundations.

## Calibration queue

- Anchor score `10` only against the guide's BB(6), Schanuel, and general
  Vojta reference, not against the fame of $P\ne NP$.
- Estimate cumulative expert-years and integer skip probabilities only after
  the logical chain is stable.
